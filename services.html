Our services

Non-emergency medical transportation in Effingham and the surrounding rural area. Every ride is scheduled by phone with a real person.

Medicaid rides

If you have Illinois Medicaid, your ride is usually booked through your transportation broker, such as MTM or Modivcare. We are a provider in these broker networks.

How to book a Medicaid ride

  • Call your broker to book your trip.
  • You can ask for Effingham Transport as your provider.
  • Questions? Call us at (217) 342-3120 and we’ll help you through it.

Private pay

Don’t have Medicaid, or need a ride Medicaid doesn’t cover? We offer private pay rides. Call for a quote. Pricing depends on distance and trip type.

Pay by card over the phone or by invoice.

Call for a quote

Recurring & dialysis trips

Dialysis, therapy, and other regular appointments. Set up your schedule once and we’ll plan your rides around it.

Hospital discharges

A ride home from the hospital for patients who don’t need an ambulance. Discharge planners can learn more here.

Service area

Based in Effingham and serving Effingham, Fayette, Clay, Coles, Marion, Jefferson, Christian, Macon, Piatt, Champaign, Vermilion, and Sangamon counties, plus longer regional trips by arrangement.

Not sure if we cover your area? Call (217) 342-3120.
