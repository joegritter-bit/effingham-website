About & Contact

A local Effingham company getting people to the medical care they need.

Our story

Effingham Transport provides non-emergency medical transportation for Effingham and the surrounding rural area. We drive Medicaid riders through broker networks including MTM and Modivcare, private pay riders, dialysis and other recurring patients, and hospital discharges.

New ownership, renewed commitment

Effingham Transport has been under new ownership since April 2026. We’re committed to the things that matter most to our riders and partners: showing up on time, treating every rider with respect, and answering the phone with a real person.
